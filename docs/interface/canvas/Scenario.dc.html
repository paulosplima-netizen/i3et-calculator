Two results are comparable only if the versions behind them are stated. That is why they are a choice, and why they travel with every exported table.

Material recipe

Receita do Projeto do Berco ao Portao (FGV/Unicamp para a Fundep, Programa Move): partiu da receita de consultoria BISD2 e ajustou a participacao de alguns materiais com informacao das montadoras brasileiras. Coluna ICEV-PBP_BISD2 do i3ET.

The two families. BISD* is the original recipe, produced by a consultancy. PBP_BISD* is the one the Projeto do Berço ao Portão built on top of it, adjusting the share of some materials with data from the Brazilian manufacturers. Same vehicle mass, different distribution among materials — and therefore different emissions.
